# The Vibration Chamber

<BlockImage id="appliedenergistics2:tile.BlockVibrationChamber" p:active="true" scale="8" />

The Vibration Chamber directly generates small to moderate amounts of [AE energy](../ae2-mechanics/energy.md). By default it produces 40 AE/t. When network storage is full it throttles down to conserve fuel, but cannot shut off completely.

## Upgrades

It supports the [Energy](upgrade_cards.md) and [Speed](upgrade_cards.md) cards. Each card increases its corresponding efficiency or burn rate by 50%, up to three cards.
